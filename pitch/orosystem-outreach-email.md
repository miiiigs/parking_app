# OROSYSTEM Outreach Draft

## Recommended Positioning

Present the project as a complementary parking innovation, not as a direct competitor.

The strongest angle is:

- your project supports the same goal of parking digitalization
- your concept can extend digital parking into better in-app session visibility, smoother payment flow, optional controlled-slot reservation, and issue handling
- you are open to collaboration, feedback, pilot discussion, or contributing to product improvements
